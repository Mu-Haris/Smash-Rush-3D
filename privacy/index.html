Privacy Policy

Smash Rush 3D · Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the Smash Rush 3D app (“the game”) uses, published by Kinex Apps (“we”, “us”). If you have any question, email muhammadharisgift1@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We don't run servers for the game. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only, so it can remember where you are:

  • Game progress: levels, coins, gems (diamonds), weapons, upgrades, unlocked areas and missions.
  • Settings: music, sound, vibration and camera sensitivity.
  • Purchase records: the App Store transaction IDs of gem packs already delivered, so a purchase is never credited twice, and the expiry date of an active Premium subscription.
  • Free gems: how many free-gem ads you've watched today, for the daily limit.

This information is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendors (IDFV);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • crash and performance information about the ads.

Google uses this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission. The first time the game starts, Apple asks whether you allow the game to track your activity. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier isn't shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad is requested. You can change them later from the game's Settings → Privacy Policy.
  • Remove ads. An active Premium subscription removes banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Purchases

Gem packs and the Premium subscription are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the game which item was bought. We don't receive your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, email us and we'll help get it removed.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays until you delete the game. We hold no game data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. For information Google has collected for ads, use the choices above or Google's tools at My Ad Center.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its use. Because we don't hold personal information ourselves, most requests are handled through the choices above or directly with Google. You can still contact us and we'll help.

Security

All connections the game makes (to Google for ads and to Apple for purchases) are encrypted.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
