Common questions

I bought gems but didn't receive them

Keep the game open with an internet connection for a moment; the game delivers any purchase the App Store confirms, even after a restart. If the purchase says “pending” (for example Ask to Buy), the gems arrive once it's approved. If they still don't arrive, email us with the order ID from Apple's receipt.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. Gem packs are consumable and can't be restored.

How do I cancel Premium?

Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Smash Rush 3D, or tap Premium Active in the game's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Premium until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change ad tracking or consent?

Tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also change your ad choices in the game's Settings → Privacy Policy. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the game removes it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game from your device. We don't keep any game data about you, and you don't need an account.
